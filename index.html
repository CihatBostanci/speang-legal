FAQ

Do I need an account? No. Speang works without sign-up, ads or purchases.

How many phones do we need? One. Pass the device around the group.

Some games say "Coming soon". Those games are still in development and will unlock in a future update.

Can I switch between English and Turkish? Yes, in the app settings. Each language has its own word and topic pool.
