Speang Privacy Policy

Effective / Yürürlük: 17 October 2026 · Speang for iOS and Android

Speang is a set of party and speaking-club games for practising English and Turkish. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases. This page explains the small amount of data the app handles.

What we collect

DataWhyLinked to you?
Crash reports (stack trace, device model, OS version, app version)Finding and fixing crashes, via Firebase CrashlyticsNo
A random anonymous app identifierDownloading game content (word lists, topics) from our server, via Firebase; basic usage statisticsNo
Usage statistics (app opens, session length, which screens are visited)Understanding how the app is used so we can improve it, via Google Analytics for FirebaseNo

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, microphone audio or advertising identifier. Player names you type into a game stay on your device.

Tracking

Speang does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not share data with data brokers or advertisers.

Third parties

The data above is processed by Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Cloud Firestore and Google Analytics for Firebase) on our behalf. See Firebase's privacy information.

Storage and deletion

Game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for up to 90 days and usage statistics by Google Analytics for up to 14 months. You can ask us to delete any data related to your device by writing to the address below.

Children

Speang is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.

Saklama ve silme

Oyun ilerlemesi ve ayarlar yalnızca cihazınızda tutulur, uygulamayı sildiğinizde silinir. Çökme raporları Crashlytics'te en fazla 90 gün, kullanım istatistikleri Google Analytics'te en fazla 14 ay saklanır. Cihazınızla ilgili verilerin silinmesini aşağıdaki adrese yazarak isteyebilirsiniz.

Çocuklar
